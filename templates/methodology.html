How recognition works

Every score, award and certification comes from verifiable data, not from a fee.

The payment score

A single score from 0 to 100 that summarises how well a company pays its suppliers.

Speed (A1)

Based on average days to pay. Full marks (100) for paying within the same day, zero for taking 50 days or more. The score falls sharply for payments beyond 30 days.

On time (A2)

The percentage of invoices paid within 30 days, taken directly from the company's filing. This sub-score rewards the businesses that actually hit the 30-day target.

On terms (A3)

Whether invoices are paid within the agreed terms. Capped at 60 if the standard terms exceed 30 days, or zero if terms exceed 60 days.

How the score is calculated

The three sub-scores (A1, A2, A3) are averaged to give the final payment score. The score is recalculated each time new filing data is published.

Where the data comes from

All figures come from the company's own mandatory filing to the UK government's payment practices register. We do not adjust or supplement them.

When scores change

Scores update whenever the government register publishes a new reporting period. Historical scores are preserved so you can see how a company has changed over time.

Recognition bands

A score threshold determines which companies qualify for a Fast Payer Award, and at which tier.

Platinum

Score 92 or above. The highest tier, awarded to companies that consistently pay suppliers very quickly and almost always within 30 days.

Gold

Score 80 to 91. Strong payment performance across all three measures. Gold companies pay quickly and reliably on the agreed terms.

Recognised

Score 50 to 79. Above-average payment performance, recognised with a Fast Payer Award. These companies pay better than most of the market.

Fast Payer Awards

Annual awards given to companies that have earned a qualifying payment score.

Who qualifies

Any company that files payment data with the UK government and whose score reaches the Recognised threshold (50 or above) in the year of the award. The award is based on the most recent 12-month reporting period.

What it means

The award year is printed on the shield. A company can hold awards from multiple years. Awards from the most recent two years appear on the company's profile as active recognition; older awards are shown for the historical record.

Accreditation and Customer of Choice

The step beyond the award, for companies that want independent proof of how they treat suppliers.

Customer of Choice diagnostic

Gold and Platinum Fast Payers who want full accreditation complete the Customer of Choice diagnostic: an independent assessment of 13 supplier practices covering everything from payment terms to communication and invoicing simplicity.

What accreditation covers

Accredited Good Business Partners have passed the diagnostic, carried a supplier survey, and had their credentials independently reviewed. The accreditation is annual and must be renewed. It cannot be bought without the diagnostic.

The data never lies

All payment figures come from mandatory government filings. A company's score, its band and its position in the directory are set only by what it reported to government. Claiming a profile or paying for accreditation cannot change any figure.
