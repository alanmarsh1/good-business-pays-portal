Data sources

This portal uses two free public data sources:

UK payment practices export
Every report filed since April 2017, covering {{ company_count | default(0) | int }} companies. Published by the Department for Business and Trade at check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk. Updated when new reports are filed.
Companies House free data product
Registered address, SIC codes, previous names and company status. Published by Companies House at download.companieshouse.gov.uk. Published monthly within 5 working days of month end.

Processing rules

All rules are applied consistently every month. Every figure on a company page can be traced to the export.

Validation
The pipeline stops if the export is missing any of its 52 expected columns, contains an unknown regime code, or has more than 5% fewer rows than the previous month's export. The live site is unchanged when the pipeline stops.
Latest report
Each company page shows the report with the latest period end date. Earlier reports feed the payment trend chart.
No-payments periods
About 9% of reports state that no payments were made in the period. These show as "No payments reported" and do not contribute to averages or benchmarks.
Outliers
Values that cannot be correct are treated as missing and logged: average days to pay above 365, and percentages outside 0 to 100.
Payment codes
The free-text payment codes field is normalised to named codes (Prompt Payment Code, Fair Payment Code, GSCOP and others), matched case-insensitively. "No" and blank mean no code membership.
Regime differences
Value-in-pounds fields appear from Regime 2 onwards. Retention details appear in Regime 3. Fields are shown only when reported.

Benchmark bands

Benchmarks use fixed bands tied to the campaign goals, so comparisons are consistent month to month.

Average time to pay

Faster than X% of companies
Calculated by comparing each company's latest average time to pay against all other companies' latest reports. This figure changes month to month as new reports are filed.

What is not shown

Company Watch risk data is not used. Standard payment period is shown as reported; it is not the average time to pay.

Licence

Payment practices data is published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Companies House data is published under the same licence.
