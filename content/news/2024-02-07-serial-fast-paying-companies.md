Yesterday [Good Business Pays CIC](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/good-business-pays/>) invited nine companies to join us at [FSB Westminster](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/fsb-westminster/>) to special event.

These companies have been paying their suppliers both FAST and ON-TIME for as long as reporting records began back in 2017. They are rare in number with less than 50 large UK companies that meet this achievement.

The Business Minister [Kevin Hollinrake](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAALYNcoBbKUWQO8nJCibmIGUU2YxK1orLJk>) handed awards to each of the nine winners alongside our CEO [Terry Corby CMgr CCMI](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAAESu4BDVvgKKS2H6g-OYG5UN4asUTIt3Q>).

Congratulations to all of the winning companies and the people behind them: [Gerry Stevens](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAK_pd4B9BjvGbW7HvMQM2wNurUILJDvjdc>) from [Groupe Clarins](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/groupe-clarins/>); [Neil Frost](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAJFuw8BB4_rMBwKS9Qq6m14KqWQekhuFoU>) at [Interflora UK & Ireland](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/interflorauk/>); [Ross Ewing](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAvYmWQBc246cKbwk9EMiO6jqcijk9aWivY>) at [McDonald's](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/mcdonald's-corporation/>); [David Anderson](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAA0thqkBq7BceN08QNNni8H3Cv0MQB8htWw>) at [The Openfield Partnership: More Than Just Grain](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/openfield-agriculture-ltd/>); [Andy Platt](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAADsX0QBBEhmJMQFCP-B2qhMZ_QcPi_a1tw>) at [Pizza Hut](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/pizza-hut/>); [Chris Cale](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACMKJ5sBzzYuhybu4JP327RR_GROSnbsSwg>) at [Simplyhealth](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/simplyhealth/>) / [Denplan](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/denplan/>); [Tony Walledge](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAABll5tABgC_W7vhM2c_6lKKg4m_0ULkdfJ0>) and @Esther Yates at [Sony Music Entertainment](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/sony-music-entertainment/>); [Ross Simpson](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACP5y4oBxWuDwDOIQAjxwMb7gVq52JWrGBM>) at [Trailfinders](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/trailfinders/>).

Also we heard the important story of how [Julianne Ponan MBE](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAVpS6YBSb4zZZWGfxLnyTMW8qAMIZFnBSo>) has grown [Creative Nature Superfoods](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/creative-nature-superfoods/>). Thanks so much to the Good Business Pays partners for helping us make this happen: [Kelly Devine](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAABXrzQBPfOuzVZCiXdT4RHxiwrBBxIkAoM>) at [Mastercard](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/mastercard/>); [Matthew Kennett](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAAx6qEBp6ywPOZaCsT1H63wp3ESMyfwg_A>) from [NatWest Group](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/natwest-group/>); [Anna Cleland](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAALwhSIBTycA8CXNg_-qiw0W556tvW-6hBs>) from [Barclays](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/barclays-bank/>); and [Rob Burlison](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAEKwmX8Bmk7CuGVj2plIGeSRsrhDLZHaaNo>) from [Intuit](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/intuit/>).

And our thanks to [Craig Beaumont](<https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAA2Q6UBKvICcQPWTddy9v3RSq7AqcHVyR4>) and the team at [FSB Westminster](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/fsb-westminster/>) for hosting the event!
