## to bring an end to slow and late payments

Small businesses know only too well the anxiety caused by slow and late payments – hitting your cashflow, hammering your plans, and, all too often, causing businesses to fold.

The FSB estimates 50,000 UK small businesses close every year due to late payments – this has to stop.

We’re campaigning to get the UK’s big businesses to change their payment practices and commit to paying their suppliers quickly – because with new instant payment technology, there is no excuse.
