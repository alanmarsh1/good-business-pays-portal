Each year, we publish the list of the fastest and slowest paying companies for each of the main industry sectors. This allows you to easily compare the recent payment performance demonstrated by the biggest companies by sector.

In each chart, you will see the average number of days a company takes to pay their suppliers. The results shown are their most recent performance figures at the beginning of January 2025.

A full list of company payment performance for each sector is available to purchase from info@goodbusinesspays.com.
