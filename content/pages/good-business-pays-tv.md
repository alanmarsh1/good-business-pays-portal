## Welcome to @GBP_TV!

@GBP_TV is our exclusive YouTube channel and podcast which aims to highlight the issues created as a result of a culture of late payment, and importantly share good practice from across industry to promote discussion, debate and so we can all learn from the best and encourage others to improve their payment culture too.
