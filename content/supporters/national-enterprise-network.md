**National Enterprise Network (NEN) was first established in 1992 and is a membership body, our members are organisations that provide enterprise business support to new, emerging and existing businesses.**

Research shows that startups and entrepreneurs who seek enterprise support are more likely to survive and thrive, NEN is home to enterprise support.

NEN showcases the importance of enterprise support providers at a national level, representing members with key stakeholders in the public and private sectors, including UK Government, banks and universities.

NEN connect, promote and help organisations that are passionate about inspiring and encouraging enterprise. These include enterprise agencies, Chambers of Commerce, local authorities and other specialist providers.
